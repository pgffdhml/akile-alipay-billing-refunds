## The refund rule is what should decide your billing cycle

Akile's refund terms are stricter than the "14 days, no questions" line you see on most Alipay-friendly hosts, and the difference is worth reading twice:

- Full refund within **24 hours of provisioning**, provided you've used **no more than 5 GB of traffic**.
- Maximum **three machines refunded per account per calendar month**, and at most one machine per node per month.
- Refunds are credited to your **Akile account balance only**. Nothing returns to Alipay.
- **Discounted plans and coupon purchases cannot be refunded at all.** Setup fees, special configuration fees and leftover prepaid balance are never returned.

That last bullet is the expensive one. A 24-hour window is enough to check that SSH works, the route behaves and the disk performs, but it is not enough to judge a server over a month of real traffic. No. Refunds go to your Akile account balance. The eligibility rules are 24 hours from provisioning, no more than 5 GB of traffic used, three machines per account per month, and no refunds at all on discounted or coupon purchases.

**Can I pay the first invoice with Alipay and renew with a card?**
All four methods are listed at account level, so the first payment method doesn't appear to lock you in. If a specific renewal invoice only shows one option, support is the place to ask before the due date.
